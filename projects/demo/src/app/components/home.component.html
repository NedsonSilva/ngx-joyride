ngx-joyride

A pure Angular directive to build web-app tours

Let your users learn how to use your application and discover your new features through a tour.

Features

  • No external dependencies, pure Angular.
  • Scrolls automatically to the target element.
  • Autofixes step position.
  • Supports custom content.
  • Multi pages navigation.
  • AOT compliant.

Options

  • Theme colour
  • Show counter
  • Show "Prev" button
  • Default step position: top
